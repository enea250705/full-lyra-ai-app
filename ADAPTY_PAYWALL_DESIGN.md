## 🔧 **IMPLEMENTATION STEPS**

1. **Go to Adapty Dashboard → Paywalls**
2. **Create New Paywall → Visual Builder**
3. **Choose "Blank" template**
4. **Add elements in this order:**
   - Header section with gradient
   - 3 plan cards
   - Footer with trust badges
5. **Apply colors and fonts above**
6. **Add your products:**
   - `lyra_pro_monthly` to Pro card
   - `lyra_premium_monthly` to Premium card
7. **Preview on mobile/tablet**
8. **Publish when ready**
